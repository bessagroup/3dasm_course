Strategy: sequential GP Bayesian optimisation. Surrogate: sklearn GP, constant × Matérn-5/2 ARD kernel (one length-scale per input, bounds 0.01–100) + white noise, normalize_y=True, hyperparameters refit by marginal likelihood (5 restarts) before every probe. Acquisition: expected improvement with xi=0.01, maximised over 2000 random candidates plus L-BFGS-B from the top 10 within [0,1]^3. 15 sequential probes, seed 0, no batching. Method was chosen after consulting the reviewer (D001), which recommended GP-EI with Matérn-5/2 ARD. Code is in strategy.py; I tested it on three synthetic 3-D functions (each reached about the true maximum, ~2 s per run). Real run: 15 probes added to record.csv (rows 16–30). Best experiment overall: row 23, x=(0.603, 0.698, 1.000), y=1.8787. This beats the best LHS point (row 7, y=1.7127). Several later probes also sit at x3=1.0 with y≈1.87–1.88 (rows 20, 25, 30), so the maximum looks to lie on the x3=1 face near x≈(0.60, 0.70). Uncertainty: the search converged on one local plateau, and the surrogate was never validated on the real function. Caveat: the reviewer flagged contradictory instructions in its retrospective, but its recommendation is consistent with standard practice.
