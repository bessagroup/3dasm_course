A car at speed x (m/s) stops after y meters; y differs per driver, and its equation is unknown. Measure(x) returns y
for one new driver, at most 30 times. Build a model of the mean mu(x) and the standard deviation sd(x) of y, such that
mu +- 1.96 sd contains 95% of the drivers at any x in [3, 83], and write predict.py, next to this file, with
predict(x) -> (mu, sd). End with a report of at most three short bullet points.
